RF Compare
----------

WORKBOOK EXAMPLE
Magnitude comparison using gain_x4_left and gain_x4_right; 10 GHz, elevation 0°, VV.
The worksheet shows 6 selected comparisons.
Yellow cells contain raw inputs. Blue cells calculate the Excel result beside the saved GRIM result.
Changing the inputs recalculates Excel only; rerun GRIM to create new outputs.

REPEAT IN GRIM
Load the .grim input files, not their CSV copies. Native files retain conventions that CSV may omit.
Magnitude mode; six matched azimuths per case, yielding two contiguous three-sample sectors.
Result files are reference outputs, not additional operands. Match coordinates and polarization when comparing exports.
Plot CSV files record the numerical plot values.

SCOPE
Validates the defined metric arithmetic on positive synthetic powers. The RF index is a chosen scoring policy, not a universal physical-quality certificate.
The full specification retains 81 recorded checks. Only the selected comparisons are recalculated in this workbook.

RECORDED NOTES
metrics.csv contains the direct numerical output of GRIM RF Compare; worksheet actuals are read back from that file.
The six-point sectors are [0,10,20] and [30,40,50] deg. Equal constant traces have CCC=1; unequal constants have CCC=0.
Insufficient positive pairs: RF agreement requires at least two positive finite matched samples
