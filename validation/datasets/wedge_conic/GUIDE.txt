Wedge Conic
-----------

WORKBOOK EXAMPLE
Waterline elevation 0 deg; 10 GHz; HH polarization.
The worksheet shows 7 selected comparisons.
Yellow cells contain raw inputs. Blue cells calculate the Excel result beside the saved GRIM result.
Changing the inputs recalculates Excel only; rerun GRIM to create new outputs.

REPEAT IN GRIM
Load the .grim input files, not their CSV copies. Native files retain conventions that CSV may omit.
Waterline: 0/90/180/270 degrees; tilted: 30-degree azimuth steps and tilts -30/-15/0/15/30 degrees; VV HH VH.
Result files are reference outputs, not additional operands. Match coordinates and polarization when comparing exports.
Plot CSV files record the numerical plot values.

SCOPE
Waterline coordinate example only. Tilted polarization mixing and sparse-angle interpolation are outside this compact sheet.
The full specification retains 28 recorded checks. Only the selected comparisons are recalculated in this workbook.

RECORDED NOTES
One nonzero tilt cannot fill conic grid: One fixed wedge tilt away from waterline traces a great circle and cannot be converted into a normal constant-elevation azimuth sweep. Supply at least two measured wedge tilts.
