-------------

WORKBOOK EXAMPLE
Extrusion length 2.5 m. Convert input_3d to dBke and input_2d to dBsm.
The worksheet shows 12 selected comparisons.
Yellow cells contain raw inputs. Blue cells calculate the Excel result beside the saved GRIM result.
Changing the inputs recalculates Excel only; rerun GRIM to create new outputs.

SCOPE
Validates implemented broadside uniform-extrusion arithmetic. Does not establish the approximation for arbitrary finite 3-D geometry.
The full specification retains 25 recorded checks. Only the selected comparisons are recalculated in this workbook.

RECORDED NOTES
Nonpositive length: length_m must be positive and finite
dBke = 10 log10(k * width), k = 2*pi*f/c. The exported linear column contains width, not k*width.
