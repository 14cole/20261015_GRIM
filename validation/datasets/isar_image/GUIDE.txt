SCOPE
Three representative raw samples are shown; the native file contains all 561. Checks cover an origin point and sampling formulas, not arbitrary images.
The full specification retains 34 recorded checks. Only the selected comparisons are recalculated in this workbook.

RECORDED NOTES
Fast PFA uses the original frequency bandwidth and small-angle aperture. Accurate PFA uses the inscribed Cartesian rectangle: Umax=fmin*sin(half aperture); Vmax=sqrt(fmax^2-Umax^2). Its resolutions and extents therefore differ.
